        Unyielding Maul

        The tribute run in Dire Maul North. The drop is 9.8%: about 10 runs on average, 23 for a 90% chance.

        Rules

        • Kill only King Gordok.
        • Off-tank Cho'Rush until the King dies.
        • Bring someone for the inner door: rogue (300 lockpicking), engineer (Large Seaforium Charge) or blacksmith (Truesilver Skeleton Key).
        • Loot Ogre Tannin inside for the ogre suit.

          Check these in game

          • The stat guide targets 440 defense. This set adds about 45 to a base of 300. I couldn't confirm where the rest comes from, so don't assume you are crit immune.
          • Tier 0.5 steps after stage 1, Wildheart drops and recipe sources come from third-party sites.
          • I didn't confirm which battlegrounds are open now.
          • The Reforged Spear's stats are unknown to me. Compare it before you pick it.
          • One site says druids need the Starbreeze Village Relic in stage 4. Another calls that unconfirmed. Follow your quest text.
